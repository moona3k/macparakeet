MacParakeet 0.8.11

Meeting recovery, dictation controls, and playback improvements.

  • Start another meeting while the previous recording finishes processing. Retry failed finalization from the saved meeting's detail view.
  • Opt-in Beta reminders offer to record supported meetings. Recording starts only when you choose Record.
  • Choose separate dictation start and stop sounds, adjust volume, or import your own. Includes original Bloom and Gong cues.
  • Optional links can start and stop dictation. Link control is off until enabled in Settings.
  • More reliable paste/shortcut interaction and interrupted streaming insertion.
  • YouTube playback supports separate video and audio streams, with preparation progress. Saved audio stays available after cancelling or a failed video load.
  • File transcription reports temporary-storage failures during audio-track discovery.
  • Improved meeting echo alignment and one corrective retry for incomplete knowledge cards.
  • Automatic meeting titles recover from transient provider failures and retain valid topic years.
  • Compact Settings layouts and vocabulary fixes, including standalone C++, C#, and .NET.
  • Bundled CLI 5.2.0 returns saved corrections in history JSON and adds the activity_detection meeting trigger value.

Upgrading from an older version also brings transcript find and replace, speaker-count selection before transcription, a second dictation shortcut, meeting microphone recovery, and lower memory use during long meetings. Apple Intelligence is available for cleanup only.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 14.2 or later.
