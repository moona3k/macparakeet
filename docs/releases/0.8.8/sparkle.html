MacParakeet 0.8.8

More dependable dictation, safer editing, and more control over your AI tools. Speech recognition stays on your Mac; cloud AI remains optional.

Dictate with fewer interruptions

  • Failed recognition keeps its recording in History for retry when Save dictation history is enabled.
  • Fixes for delayed Fn releases, rapid restart, stale cancellation, and Stop waiting on stalled live recognition.
  • Optional shortcuts to copy without pasting or AI-polish one dictation. AI polish requires AI Formatter and a configured provider.
  • Optional capture sounds, top/bottom pill placement, and a choice to let Escape pass through. Say “question mark” or “exclamation mark” in Clean mode.

Edit and organize your Library

  • Correct or omit passages in the transcript reading view, and edit saved summaries and action lists directly, safe against stale-draft overwrites and misread deletions after a reload; if a result is genuinely removed elsewhere, Copy Draft copies your unsaved edit to the clipboard while Discard Draft abandons it.
  • More responsive long transcripts, reachable editing controls, roomier Notes, and a tighter Meetings overview.
  • Manage Prompts from the Library toolbar. Run saved Transforms from the menu bar.
  • Safer transcript saves, cancellable folder imports, and recording recovery that preserves uncertain audio and cleared notes.

Choose the tools for each job

  • Separate AI provider/model routes for cleanup and analysis; choose the output language of meeting AI results.
  • Optional on-device Apple Intelligence on eligible Macs with macOS 26+. Best for short text; large AI Formatter requests fall back to deterministic cleanup.
  • Four-step onboarding with shortcut rehearsal and practice dictation; optional Orukeet preview; replace-all vocabulary import.
  • Automatic speaker detection now uses Nemotron, up to eight speakers per source. An additional model download may be needed before offline use. Explicit speaker counts continue to use Community-1.
  • Bundled CLI 4.7.0 adds saved-result editing, batch transcript corrections, and shared AI routes.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 14.2+. Jev Voice Control and the new cross-recording Ask workspace remain experimental and disabled in release builds.
