A reminder to record the call you just joined.

Ad-hoc calls do not always appear in the calendar. An opt-in local reminder offers to record a detected meeting, while the user decides when capture begins.

Opt-in BetaOff by defaultRecord / Not nowLocal metadataNo automatic recording

The user experience

Enable Settings → Capture → Meetings → Remind me to record meetings. Three seconds of qualifying activity makes a call eligible for a nonactivating, keyless reminder. The setting is labelled Beta.

Record rechecks the current call and uses the normal recording and permission flow, so choosing it may bring up the recording panel and any permission prompts. Not now suppresses the reminder for that call.

Prompts wait while dictation or recording is busy, including recorder startup and finalization. Record and Not now remain separate Accessibility controls. The reminder itself leaves keyboard focus in the meeting app and does not intercept Return or Escape; focus can move only after you choose Record. The feature does not automatically record a call.

From activity to an offer

  1. 01 · ObserveRead local process audio-use flags and bounded browser Accessibility metadata.
  2. 02 · IdentifyResolve a native app or a recognized browser room; exclude observed prejoin screens.
  3. 03 · WaitRequire a stable three-second dwell and an available dictation/recording flow.
  4. 04 · AskShow Record / Not now. Recheck fresh evidence before accepting Record.
The timer reconciles unchanged signals, so a stable microphone does not need to emit another event when the dwell completes.

One call, one reminder

Distinct browser rooms have separate identities. A shown or dismissed browser call keeps its suppression when a background tab stops exposing metadata, while the browser remains running.

Unknown metadata cannot authorize an old Record action. During a brief observation gap, the panel can remain visible with Record disabled and Not now available. An explicit prejoin/end observation resets the room; a later joined call can qualify again. Native process exit or PID replacement resets suppression; otherwise input absence uses a 30-second grace period.

Prejoin exclusions come from the current snapshot. A previously observed Zoom or Teams preview therefore cannot permanently veto the subsequent joined call. Successful capture consumes the confirmed room or sole observed candidate; failed/cancelled starts and ambiguous concurrent calls keep their eligibility.

Local observation

Detection reads process audio-use flags, browser document URLs, and selected accessible button names. It does not capture audio, camera frames, transcripts, or page text for detection.

Browser URLs and labels stay local and are not sent as telemetry. Accepted recordings use the additive activity_detection trigger attribution.

Calendar automation and existing dictation behavior retain their own controls. A legacy experimental activity auto-start preference resolves to prompt mode.
