## What was wrong in the v1 grid

The shipping recipe was Recursive Canopy: 5–6 roots, four generations,
three palette families (Tidal / Lichen / Plum), and a gold nucleus on
every card.

At library scale that read as:

1. **Weak structure.** Primary length ~16% of the card. Limb fills faded
   from 22% to 72% opacity. Fine forks vanished.
2. **Two colors too loud.** Every family carried a warm gold `accent`.
   The nucleus was 92% opaque. That gold was a second product color.
3. **Not uniform.** Teal and plum were equally likely. Side by side they
   looked like two different apps. YouTube cards then won on contrast.

Brand coral is meant for attention (recording, CTAs), not wallpaper.

## Decision

**Seed of Life, Present scale.** Seven equal circles. The UUID chooses
rotation, which one or two rings are lit, a 1% center drift, radius
jitter, and sage ink ±12°. Background stays night. No gold, no second
family, no branches.

v2 is an explicit recipe replacement. UUID plus version seed the cover,
so items reshape once at this cutover and then stay put.
