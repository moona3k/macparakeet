## Procedure and results

The app was built and signed with `scripts/dev/run_app.sh`. This environment
needed `GIT_EXEC_PATH=/opt/homebrew/opt/git/libexec/git-core` for Xcode's Git
submodule helper. Native Accessibility menu actions started and stopped
recordings. A known 14.021-second fixture generated by `say -v Samantha -r 145`
played through the MacBook Pro speakers into the MacBook Pro microphone.
The device settings remained at output volume 31 and input volume 60; only
`afplay`'s per-player gain changed. Both devices operated at 48 kHz.

Every capture reported `healthy`, with no interrupted source or capture
failure. Selected raw tracks and playback artifacts decode. The microphone-only
capture leaves an empty, unselected system-track placeholder; it is not counted
as a captured or failed source. Each recording retained its transcript JSON,
Markdown, manifest, capture metadata and playback audio.

The first run is retained as an unsuccessful recognition probe. Normalized
cross-correlation found the known fixture in the system recording at 31.802 s
(peak 0.516) and in the microphone recording at 31.980 s (peak 0.270). This
supports delivery through both audio paths despite the absent recognized
phrase. Increasing the player gain and shortening the recording yielded the
successful repeat. Other playback was not controlled, so this is evidence of
conditions affecting recognition, not an isolated root-cause diagnosis or an
accuracy benchmark.

## What remains unqualified

- Both dual-source runs reported `rawNoAECAssets`. These runs do not qualify
  acoustic echo cancellation. The microphone-only run reported
  `rawMissingSystemReference`, consistent with its selected source mode.
- Stored speaker counts were three, three and one. These are the product's
  current roster counts, which can include source-only buckets; they are not
  ground-truth counts of people in this uncontrolled playback setting.
- No Bluetooth device, route transition, long call, external microphone,
  real multi-person room or constrained-memory workload was tested.
- No microphone/screen permission reset, first-run onboarding, actual dictation
  insertion into another app, clean-room word error rate or release claim
  follows from these checks.

The next diarization work remains the durable outcome/provenance slice and a
held-out evaluation of final attributed words. Physical frame delivery alone
cannot establish speaker identity accuracy.
