Preserve the foundation.
Prove the whole experience.

The Core / ViewModels / app / CLI architecture is worth keeping. The audit reproduced defects in data ownership and setup recovery, and includes narrow fixes. The next investment should establish who said what and whether the complete first-use journey works.

Scope: CLI, GUI, onboarding, accessibility, telemetry, architecture, test coverage, CI, performance, and speaker diarization.

Concrete fixes included

Protect current user intent

  • CLI retranscription preserves Core’s committed metadata and rejects deleted or changed-status dictation rows.
  • Library mutations retire stale refreshes while preserving pagination.
  • Setup failures keep readable guidance and reachable recovery controls.
  • Onboarding dashboard correction is prepared in the receiver repository.

These are audit changes. No release, production deployment, or merge is implied.

Five priorities, in order

Close the smallest meaningful evidence gap before changing another default.

  1. Make speaker attribution explainable

    Persist detection outcome, actual backend, model, and fallback. Test the final words against held-out reference speakers, including short replies and unknown intervals.

  2. Qualify the first successful journey

    Use a disposable macOS account for permissions, download, real practice, paste, saved History, and relaunch. Give Whisper the same background-setup lifetime and stall recovery contract.

  3. Close the observability contract

    Make compatibility checks execute in first-party CI. Pair producer and receiver revisions, complete crash ingestion validation, and verify the corrected onboarding aggregation after an authorized deployment.

  4. Measure the difficult workload

    Profile cold starts, long meetings, video-call contention, and low-memory Macs. Opt-in stop-to-posted-paste timing had a 6.645-second p99 and 225 ms median; neither proves insertion in the destination app.

  5. Reduce CI wait and concentrated ownership

    The measured 35m02s baseline was dominated by Release/Bundle work. Investigate cache usefulness before removing tests. Extract one cohesive workflow at a time from large views.

Regression evidence, not general accuracy

The pinned Nemotron model card lists VoxConverse development and test recordings in training. These two cases are therefore not a held-out quality set.

Nemotron improves both cases, yet still predicts an extra speaker on ouvtt. The result supports the current default, not a universal accuracy claim or another threshold change.

The final transcript has another failure boundary. On one real fixed-ASR crop, smoothing reduced correct attribution from 487 to 486 of 491 eligible word midpoints.
