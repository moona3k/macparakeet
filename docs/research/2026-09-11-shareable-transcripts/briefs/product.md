# Product and market workstream

Read `shared.md` first.

## One goal

Identify the smallest share experience that feels unusually trustworthy and useful for MacParakeet, grounded in current comparable products and real recipient/owner workflows.

## Questions to answer

- How do Granola, Otter, Fireflies, Fathom, Descript, Notion, Google Docs, Dropbox, and privacy-oriented tools expose link sharing, expiry, access control, indexing, duplication, and revocation today?
- Which controls belong in the creation flow versus the post-share management surface?
- What should the recipient page include for a transcript, meeting notes, summaries, chapters, timestamps, speaker labels, provenance, and source context?
- What are the minimum useful analytics, if any, that do not betray MacParakeet's privacy posture?
- Which adjacent ideas strengthen the private speech-memory strategy, and which would turn the product into an overbroad cloud collaboration suite?

## Done

Evidence covers at least five direct or adjacent products using official documentation or the live product's public surfaces, and ends with a phased product recommendation plus concrete user flows and anti-goals.
